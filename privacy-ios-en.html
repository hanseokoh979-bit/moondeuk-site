Privacy Policy (iPhone)

Last updated: November 16, 2026 · 한국어 · 日本語

Moondeuk reads where and when the photos in your library were taken to build a travel map, and makes a short vlog for each trip. This page lists, without exception, what Moondeuk for iPhone reads, where it keeps it, and when anything leaves your device. If this English translation and the Korean original differ, the Korean original applies.

Your travel records stay on this device. The developer neither receives nor keeps the travel records made in the app. There are no accounts to sign up for.

Only when you use friend rooms are the photos you pick and the captions you write stored on a server run by Yeoul. Photos, captions, room names, and your display name are locked with the room key on this device before they are sent, so the server and the developer cannot open them. The key exists only in the invite link. Photo locations are not sent (section 3 ⑤).

Your original photo files never leave your device. They are not included in backups either. When you share or post to a friend room, a newly drawn copy is sent instead of the original, with no photo location or device information (section 3 ④ and ⑤).

No ads, no analytics, and no tracking. The app's data is not sold or used for advertising.

The app sends data off your device in five cases, all listed in section 3.

2. Where your data is stored

By default, on this device only, in storage that only Moondeuk can read.

Only if you use Moondeuk's backup, a copy of your travel records is also stored in your own iCloud, in a private area for this app that other apps cannot read — and neither can the developer.

A backup contains:

  • Trips (dates, names, hidden and locked flags), cities (name, country, representative coordinates), and songs you saved in an earlier version or in the Android version (included as they are, if any remain)
  • For each photo on your map: capture time and coordinates, references used to find the photo again (photo library ID, iCloud Photos identifier, original file name), and trash flags
  • Settings — your home area radius and coordinates of places you marked "Not Home" or added as home (plus the music app you chose in an earlier version or in the Android version, if any)

Not included: original photos, photo images, globe stickers, Filter Photos, vlogs, or widget copies. Filter Photos are saved to your photo library as new photos; Moondeuk keeps only their library IDs on this device. A globe sticker is a small copy of a photo's subject, cut out on this device. A vlog is made on the device the first time you open it and kept in a temporary folder (cache) on this device to be reused. iOS may clear it when storage runs low, and Delete All Records erases it. The list of photos you pick for a vlog and the list of photos you dim on the globe also stay on this device. Any video of you that an earlier version (1.3.7) copied into the app's own storage on this device for the first scene of a vlog is erased the first time you open a version without that feature.

If you use friend rooms: photos and captions posted to a room are stored in locked form on the friend room server run by Yeoul (Microsoft Azure, Seoul, South Korea) (section 3 ⑤). On this device, Moondeuk keeps the room keys for the rooms you are in and an anonymous number and secret value identifying this device in the iOS Keychain (a this-device-only item), the room photos it receives and unlocks in a temporary folder (cache), and the list of people you hid and your display name in the app's own storage. None of these is included in Moondeuk backups. Delete All Room Data (top right of the Friends tab) or Delete All Records erases them, together with your photos on the server (section 8).

To show Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, Moondeuk keeps small, downsized copies of a few photos from the trips each day will show over the coming 30 days, along with the city name and trip dates and a globe image drawn by the app, in an app group container on this device (a place only Moondeuk and its widgets can read). Locked and hidden trips are not included. These are erased by Delete All Records and are not included in backups.

iPhone backups: if iCloud Backup or computer backups are on for your iPhone, the app's data on this device may be included in those iOS device backups. They are separate from Moondeuk's backup and are managed through your Apple Account and device settings. The developer cannot see them.

3. When data leaves your device — five cases

① Finding place names (everyone)

Coordinates alone don't say "Busan" or "Kyoto", so Moondeuk asks Apple's location service (geocoding) about representative coordinates.

  • It does not ask about each photo — one representative coordinate per city
  • In South Korea, if photos grouped as one city span two cities or counties, it also asks about one representative coordinate per area of about 5 km where photos gather, so region names match where the photos were taken. Each area is asked only once and remembered on the device
  • Places detected as your home area are also asked about once each, to show a neighborhood name. Places in the list of major world cities built into the app are not asked about; the list's name is used
  • City names on tickets in the Globe tab, in vlogs, and in widgets are written in English, so the first time one is drawn, Moondeuk asks once more for the English name, using the same representative coordinate (cities in the built-in list and names already received in English are not asked about)
  • Names, once received, are kept on the device and not asked again
  • No photos are sent
  • This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy

② Maps in trip details and on the back of tickets

When you open a trip or city, an Apple map appears at the top. When you tap Show Location on a ticket on Home, an Apple map of the area around where that photo was taken appears. To draw these maps, map tiles for the visible area are downloaded from Apple, so Apple can tell which area's map was requested.

  • Photo markers are placed on the map on the device — no photos are sent
  • The globes in the Globe tab, vlogs, and widgets are drawn from map data built into the app. Drawing the globe uses no network (only English city names are asked about, as in ①)
  • This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy

③ iCloud backup (only if you back up)

The contents described in section 2 are sent to your own iCloud. This includes the coordinates of places you traveled. The app talks to iCloud directly, never through a developer server.

  • Backups are not automatic. One is uploaded each time you tap Back Up Now in Me › Backup
  • Restore downloads the backup from your iCloud and replaces the records on this device
  • When you first open Moondeuk on a new iPhone, it checks once whether your iCloud has a Moondeuk backup, and if so asks whether to restore it

④ Sharing (only when you tap Share)

When you tap Share, an image or video newly drawn on the device is handed to the iOS share sheet, and the app you choose (Messages, social apps, Save Image, and so on) receives it. There are two things you can share:

  • Vlog (from Vlog in trip details, or ▶ in city details) — photos from that trip or city, the moving part of Live Photos, videos, the city name (in English), and the city's place on a globe drawn by the app. No dates are shown and there is no sound
  • Year in Review — two images: a cover with eight photos from that year's trips, and a closing image with the year and up to six photos cut into country shapes

For both:

  • No photo location (GPS) or other original metadata is included. It is a newly drawn image or video, not the original file. Capture time is shown only to the day
  • Photos, city names, and routes of locked trips are never included
  • Moondeuk does not send it anywhere itself or keep a copy. How the receiving app handles it follows that app's policy

⑤ Friend rooms (only if you create or join one)

In the Friends tab you can create a room with friends you traveled with and send an invite link, or join a room through a link a friend sent you, and then add the photos you liked from that trip for each other. Rooms go through a server run by Yeoul (Microsoft Azure cloud, Seoul, South Korea region). There are no accounts; the first time you use rooms, this device creates an anonymous number for itself. Friend rooms are only for people aged 14 and older, and the app asks the first time you create or join a room (section 10).

  • Photos are never posted automatically. Photos are posted only when you tap Add Photos in a room, pick photos, and tap Post
  • Everything is locked before it is sent. Each room has a room key (AES-256) created on the device, and the items below are locked with it before they are sent. The key exists only after the # in the invite link and is never sent to the server, so the server and the developer cannot open the locked contents
    • The photos you pick, redrawn as JPEGs with a long side of 1600 px (2560 px with Premium) — not the original files, and with no photo location or device information
    • The short caption you add (up to 150 characters), and the photo's capture time and aspect ratio
    • The room name and the display name shown to the people in the room
  • Not sent: photo locations (coordinates), city names, travel records, or any other photos in your library. The year sections in a room and the city name in its vlog are added by each person's device from its own records. The scenery in a photo may still reveal a place, so we recommend not posting photos that show your home or where you are staying
  • What the server can see (not locked) — this device's anonymous number and a hash of its secret value, the room number and a hash of the room access value, which device is in which room, which device posted which photo and when, the size of the locked files, how many photos were posted each day (to enforce limits), and the IP address used to connect (it may remain in cloud logs). No name, contact details, or Apple Account is collected
  • Only people with the invite link can see the room. The link is the room key, so please send it only to the people you traveled with. The link is sent through the app you choose (Messages, KakaoTalk, and so on), and how that app handles it follows that app's policy. Anyone the link is passed on to can also join
  • People in a room see each other's display names and posted photos and captions. They can open a photo and save it to their own library, and we cannot stop someone from taking a screenshot
  • When you take a photo down, it is deleted from the server right away. If you leave a room, the photos you posted are deleted too, and when no one is left in a room, the whole room is deleted. You can Hide a friend's photos on this device only (the person is not notified)
  • Report — if a photo a friend added is a problem, open it, then tap ⋯ › Report to email the developer. Because room photos are locked and the developer can't open them on the server, the photo as opened on your device, plus the name of the person who added it, its note, the room number and the photo number, are attached to the email (the room key is not). You can review, change or cancel it in the mail window before sending. Reports are handled like inquiries in section 11, and photos that break the rules are deleted from the server
  • Deleted items stay on the server in locked form so accidental deletions can be recovered, and are permanently deleted within two months of deletion (30 days as a previous version, then 30 days in the recycle bin). They remain locked during that time, so the server and the developer cannot open them
  • Limits — 3 MB per photo, 500 photos and 20 people per room, 30 photos (200 with Premium) per person per room, and 60 photos and 30 rooms per device per day
  • Running the server is entrusted to Microsoft (section 7). The app talks to the server only over an encrypted connection (HTTPS)

These five are all the app sends. The only things stored on the developer's side are the locked photos and captions in friend rooms, the information listed in ⑤, and what you choose to send when you contact us (section 11).

4. Purchases

Moondeuk Premium (monthly and yearly subscriptions, and Lifetime) is paid through the App Store and processed by Apple.

  • Payment methods and details are handled by Apple; Moondeuk never receives them
  • The app only checks, on the device, which product you have from the purchase records Apple provides
  • The developer receives only sales totals from Apple — not who bought (name, contact, or Apple Account)
  • You can cancel subscriptions in iPhone Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions
  • Refunds are handled by Apple. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com

5. On This Day notifications

A feature that sends you, in the evening, a photo from a trip years ago today (or that week). Tapping it opens that trip's vlog.

  • These are local notifications scheduled on the device. There is no server sending them
  • The small photo in a notification is a reduced copy made on the device and kept with iOS notifications. It may appear on the Lock Screen
  • Photos from locked trips are never chosen for notifications. From version 1.3.2, notifications you already received are also removed if you lock that trip later
  • Turn them off in Me › On This Day Notifications, or in Settings › Apps › Moondeuk › Notifications

6. Trip Lock and device authentication

When you lock a trip, opening it requires Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode.

  • iOS performs the check. The app only learns "passed / not passed" and cannot access face or fingerprint data
  • Moondeuk never creates or stores a separate password — with no stored secret, there is nothing to leak
  • A locked trip leaves only a lock placeholder on Home, with no photo or name, and does not appear on the Globe or in notifications, widgets, share images, vlogs, or counts

7. Third parties and outsourced processing

Data is not provided to third parties.

  • Moondeuk contains no advertising SDKs, analytics tools, or crash reporters, and does not track you across other apps or websites
  • Data is not sold or shared
  • Cases ①–④ in section 3 go to Apple's services, your own iCloud, or apps you choose; the developer never receives your data. Apple's servers may be located outside your country
  • Photos and captions you post to a friend room are seen by the people in the room you invited or were invited to. This is sharing you choose, one photo at a time; it is not provided by the developer
  • Messages sent to our contact email are received through Google's Gmail (section 11)
  • If you turned on "Share With App Developers" in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements, Apple may pass crash logs to the developer. These contain no photos or travel records, and nothing is passed if you turn it off

Outsourced processing — Running and storing the friend room server is entrusted to Microsoft Korea, Inc. (Microsoft Azure cloud). The work entrusted is running the server and storing locked files, and the storage location is South Korea (Seoul region). Photos, captions, and names are entrusted in locked form, so Microsoft cannot open them either. When you delete rooms or records, they are deleted as described in section 3 ⑤.

What is on the friend room server is kept until you take it down, leave the room, or use Delete All Room Data below, and is permanently deleted within two months of deletion (section 3 ⑤). 10. Children's privacy

Anyone can use Moondeuk, with no age limit (App Store age rating: 4+). Travel records stay on the device and do not reach the developer.

The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14. If you are under 14, please contact us together with a parent or guardian.

Friend rooms are only for people aged 14 and older, because your name and the photos and captions you pick are stored locked on the server and seen by the people in the room (section 3 ⑤). The app asks whether you are 14 or older the first time you create or join a room, and you cannot use friend rooms if you are not.

12. Your rights

  • The app's records and the photos you posted to friend rooms can be viewed and deleted by you directly in the app (section 8). The developer cannot open the locked contents of friend rooms, so viewing or correcting them is also possible only in the app
  • For contact information the developer holds, you can ask by email to access, correct, or delete it, or to stop processing it. We answer within 10 days of receiving your request
  • We confirm it's you by corresponding with the email address you contacted us from. We do not ask for ID
  • If the law requires us to keep a record, we tell you why and delete it when the period ends

13. How your information is protected

  • The app's records are kept in the private storage iOS gives each app
  • Photos, captions, names, and capture times in friend rooms are locked on this device with the room key (AES-256-GCM) before they are sent, and the key is never sent to the server. The server keeps only a hash of the value that decides who can enter a room, and only a hash of each device's secret value. Room keys and the device secret are kept in the iOS Keychain (this device only)
  • The app talks to the server only over HTTPS (TLS 1.2 or later). The server's storage cannot be opened directly from outside, and accidental deletions can be recovered for a while (everything is permanently deleted within two months)
  • Moondeuk contains no advertising, analytics, or other third-party SDKs
  • Locked trips open only with device authentication (section 6)
  • The contact email account is protected with two-step verification

14. Cookies and automatic collection

  • The app does not use cookies or advertising identifiers. Settings stay on this device
  • This web page does not use cookies or analytics tools. Its host, GitHub Pages, logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, under GitHub's policy
