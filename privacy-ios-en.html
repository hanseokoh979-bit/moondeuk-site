Privacy Policy (iPhone)

Last updated: November 7, 2026 · 한국어 · 日本語

Moondeuk reads where and when the photos in your library were taken to build a travel map, and makes a short vlog for each trip. This page lists, without exception, what Moondeuk for iPhone reads, where it keeps it, and when anything leaves your device. If this English translation and the Korean original differ, the Korean original applies.

Moondeuk has no servers. The developer neither receives nor keeps the travel records made in the app. There are no accounts to sign up for. The only thing the developer receives is what you choose to send when you contact us (section 11).

Your original photos never leave your device. They are not included in backups either. Only when you tap Share does a newly drawn image or video go to the app you choose — not the original file, and not its location (section 3 ④).

Only when you post a ticket to a friends group does a newly drawn ticket image go to the people in that group, together with the spot where the photo was taken. Before it is posted, you are shown that spot on a map and asked to confirm (section 3 ⑤).

No ads, no analytics, and no tracking. The app's data is not passed to any third party.

The app sends data off your device in five cases, all listed in section 3. None of them send anything to the developer.

2. Where your data is stored

By default, on this device only, in storage that only Moondeuk can read.

Only if you use Moondeuk's backup, a copy of your travel records is also stored in your own iCloud, in a private area for this app that other apps cannot read — and neither can the developer.

A backup contains:

  • Trips (dates, names, hidden and locked flags), cities (name, country, representative coordinates), and songs you saved in an earlier version or in the Android version (included as they are, if any remain)
  • For each photo on your map: capture time and coordinates, references used to find the photo again (photo library ID, iCloud Photos identifier, original file name), and trash flags
  • Settings — your home area radius and coordinates of places you marked "Not Home" or added as home (plus the music app you chose in an earlier version or in the Android version, if any)

Not included: original photos, photo images, globe stickers, Filter Photos, vlogs, or widget copies. Filter Photos are saved to your photo library as new photos; Moondeuk keeps only their library IDs on this device. A globe sticker is a small copy of a photo's subject, cut out on this device. A vlog is made on the device the first time you open it and kept in a temporary folder (cache) on this device to be reused. iOS may clear it when storage runs low, and Delete All Records erases it. The list of photos you pick for a vlog and the list of photos you dim on the globe also stay on this device. Any video of you that an earlier version (1.3.7) copied into the app's own storage on this device for the first scene of a vlog is erased the first time you open a version without that feature.

If you are in a friends group: tickets posted to the group (image, city, country, spot where the photo was taken, and time posted) are stored in the iCloud of the person who created the group (in the space this app shares with the group, within its own area of iCloud) and use that person's iCloud storage. The developer cannot read them. On this device, Moondeuk keeps the ticket images it receives in a temporary folder (cache) and records which of your photos you posted and the people you hid in the app's own storage. None of these is included in Moondeuk backups, and both are erased by Delete All Records (the group and its tickets in iCloud remain — see section 8).

To show Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, Moondeuk keeps small, downsized copies of a few photos from the trips each day will show over the coming 30 days, along with the city name and trip dates and a globe image drawn by the app, in an app group container on this device (a place only Moondeuk and its widgets can read). Locked and hidden trips are not included. These are erased by Delete All Records and are not included in backups.

iPhone backups: if iCloud Backup or computer backups are on for your iPhone, the app's data on this device may be included in those iOS device backups. They are separate from Moondeuk's backup and are managed through your Apple Account and device settings. The developer cannot see them.

3. When data leaves your device — five cases

① Finding place names (everyone)

Coordinates alone don't say "Busan" or "Kyoto", so Moondeuk asks Apple's location service (geocoding) about representative coordinates.

  • It does not ask about each photo — one representative coordinate per city
  • In South Korea, if photos grouped as one city span two cities or counties, it also asks about one representative coordinate per area of about 5 km where photos gather, so region names match where the photos were taken. Each area is asked only once and remembered on the device
  • Places detected as your home area are also asked about once each, to show a neighborhood name. Places in the list of major world cities built into the app are not asked about; the list's name is used
  • City names on tickets in the Globe tab, in vlogs, and in widgets are written in English, so the first time one is drawn, Moondeuk asks once more for the English name, using the same representative coordinate (cities in the built-in list and names already received in English are not asked about)
  • Names, once received, are kept on the device and not asked again
  • No photos are sent
  • This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy

② Maps in trip details and on the back of tickets

When you open a trip or city, an Apple map appears at the top. When you tap Show Location on a ticket on Home, an Apple map of the area around where that photo was taken appears. To draw these maps, map tiles for the visible area are downloaded from Apple, so Apple can tell which area's map was requested.

  • Photo markers are placed on the map on the device — no photos are sent
  • The globes in the Globe tab, vlogs, and widgets are drawn from map data built into the app. Drawing the globe uses no network (only English city names are asked about, as in ①)
  • This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy

③ iCloud backup (only if you back up)

The contents described in section 2 are sent to your own iCloud. This includes the coordinates of places you traveled. The app talks to iCloud directly, never through a developer server.

  • Backups are not automatic. One is uploaded each time you tap Back Up Now in Me › Backup
  • Restore downloads the backup from your iCloud and replaces the records on this device
  • When you first open Moondeuk on a new iPhone, it checks once whether your iCloud has a Moondeuk backup, and if so asks whether to restore it

④ Sharing (only when you tap Share)

When you tap Share, an image or video newly drawn on the device is handed to the iOS share sheet, and the app you choose (Messages, social apps, Save Image, and so on) receives it. There are two things you can share:

  • Vlog (from Vlog in trip details, or ▶ in city details) — photos from that trip or city, the moving part of Live Photos, videos, the city name (in English), and the city's place on a globe drawn by the app. No dates are shown and there is no sound
  • Year in Review — two images: a cover with eight photos from that year's trips, and a closing image with the year and up to six photos cut into country shapes

For both:

  • No photo location (GPS) or other original metadata is included. It is a newly drawn image or video, not the original file. Capture time is shown only to the day
  • Photos, city names, and routes of locked trips are never included
  • Moondeuk does not send it anywhere itself or keep a copy. How the receiving app handles it follows that app's policy

⑤ Friends groups (only if you create or join one)

In the Friends tab you can create a group and send an invite link, or join a group through a link a friend sent you, and then share travel tickets with the people in it. Groups work through Apple's iCloud sharing. Nothing passes through a developer server, and the developer cannot see the contents. Everyone in a group must be signed in to iCloud.

  • Tickets are never posted automatically. A ticket is posted, one at a time, only when you tap Share with Friends on the screen that opens when you tap a photo in the Globe tab, check the spot on the map, and tap Share This Location
  • What is posted — one ticket image newly drawn on the device (the photo, the city and country names in English, the trip's dates and length, and the day the photo was taken), the city name and country, the coordinates of the spot where the photo was taken (the city's representative coordinates if the photo has no location), and the time it was posted
  • The spot is sent as exact coordinates. People in the group can look at it closely on a satellite map. We recommend not posting photos taken near your home or where you are staying
  • Your original photo and its original metadata are not sent — the image file is newly drawn, so it carries no photo location or device information (the coordinates are sent separately, as above)
  • Only people invited into that group can see it. The person who created the group can invite and remove people, up to 100 per group. People in the group can see each other's Apple Account names, and Apple's sharing window shows information about the people in the group as Apple determines
  • Tickets are stored in the iCloud of the person who created the group (section 2). Invite links are sent through the app you choose (Messages, Mail, and so on)
  • When you take a ticket down, it is removed from the group right away. If you leave a group, your tickets are taken down too, and if the creator deletes the group, the group and all its tickets are deleted. However, we cannot stop someone in the group who has already seen it from taking a screenshot
  • Photos from locked or hidden trips cannot be posted (they do not appear in the Globe tab)
  • If you tap Report on a friend's ticket, your mail app opens with a message to the developer containing that ticket's city, country, poster's name, and ticket ID (it reaches the developer only if you send it — section 11; the ticket image and the spot are not included). Hide This Person's Tickets is recorded only on this device, and the person is not notified
  • This processing is done by Apple and is governed by Apple's privacy policy

These five are all the app sends. The only thing that reaches the developer is what you choose to send when you contact us (section 11).

4. Purchases

Moondeuk Premium (monthly and yearly subscriptions, and Lifetime) is paid through the App Store and processed by Apple.

  • Payment methods and details are handled by Apple; Moondeuk never receives them
  • The app only checks, on the device, which product you have from the purchase records Apple provides
  • The developer receives only sales totals from Apple — not who bought (name, contact, or Apple Account)
  • You can cancel subscriptions in iPhone Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions
  • Refunds are handled by Apple. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com

5. On This Day notifications

A feature that sends you, in the evening, a photo from a trip years ago today (or that week). Tapping it opens that trip's vlog.

  • These are local notifications scheduled on the device. There is no server sending them
  • The small photo in a notification is a reduced copy made on the device and kept with iOS notifications. It may appear on the Lock Screen
  • Photos from locked trips are never chosen for notifications. From version 1.3.2, notifications you already received are also removed if you lock that trip later
  • Turn them off in Me › On This Day Notifications, or in Settings › Apps › Moondeuk › Notifications

6. Trip Lock and device authentication

When you lock a trip, opening it requires Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode.

  • iOS performs the check. The app only learns "passed / not passed" and cannot access face or fingerprint data
  • Moondeuk never creates or stores a separate password — with no stored secret, there is nothing to leak
  • A locked trip leaves only a lock placeholder on Home, with no photo or name, and does not appear on the Globe or in notifications, widgets, share images, vlogs, friends groups, or counts

7. Third parties

None.

  • Moondeuk contains no advertising SDKs, analytics tools, or crash reporters, and does not track you across other apps or websites
  • Data is not sold or shared
  • Cases ①–⑤ in section 3 go to Apple's services, your own iCloud, apps you choose, or friends groups you choose; the developer never receives your data. Apple's servers may be located outside your country
  • Tickets and spots you post to a friends group are seen by the people in the group you invited or were invited to. This is sharing you choose, one ticket at a time; it is not provided by the developer
  • Messages sent to our contact email are received through Google's Gmail (section 11)
  • If you turned on "Share With App Developers" in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements, Apple may pass crash logs to the developer. These contain no photos or travel records, and nothing is passed if you turn it off

10. Children's privacy

Anyone can use Moondeuk, with no age limit (App Store age rating: 4+). The app's records stay on the device and do not reach the developer.

The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14. If you are under 14, please contact us together with a parent or guardian.

Friends groups include the spot where each photo was taken (section 3 ⑤). If you are under 14, please use friends groups together with a parent or guardian.
