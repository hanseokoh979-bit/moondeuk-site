Privacy Policy (iPhone)

Last updated: October 25, 2026 · 한국어 · 日本語

Moondeuk reads where and when the photos in your library were taken to build a travel map, and makes a short video for each trip. This page lists, without exception, what Moondeuk for iPhone reads, where it keeps it, and when anything leaves your device. If this English translation and the Korean original differ, the Korean original applies.

Moondeuk has no servers. The developer keeps none of your data. There are no accounts to sign up for.

Your original photos never leave your device. They are not included in backups either. Only when you tap Share does a newly drawn image or video go to the app you choose — not the original file, and not its location (section 3 ④).

No ads, no analytics, and no tracking. No data is passed to any third party.

Data leaves your device in four cases, all listed in section 3. None of them send anything to the developer.

  • Moondeuk never moves, deletes, or changes your photos. The only things it adds to your library are one new photo when you tap Save in Filter Photos and one new video when you tap Save on a trip video — the originals stay as they are, and new photos and videos do not include a location (for Filter Photos, only the date taken is kept the same as the original).

2. Where your data is stored

By default, on this device only, in storage that only Moondeuk can read.

Only if you back up, a copy of your travel records is also stored in your own iCloud, in a private area for this app that other apps cannot read — and neither can the developer.

A backup contains:

  • Trips (dates, names, hidden and locked flags), cities (name, country, representative coordinates), and songs you saved in an earlier version or in the Android version (included as they are, if any remain)
  • For each photo on your map: capture time and coordinates, references used to find the photo again (photo library ID, iCloud Photos identifier, original file name), and trash flags
  • Settings — your home area radius and coordinates of places you marked "Not Home" or added as home (plus the music app you chose in an earlier version or in the Android version, if any)

Not included: original photos, photo images, globe stickers, Filter Photos, trip videos, or widget copies. Filter Photos are saved to your photo library as new photos; Moondeuk keeps only their library IDs on this device so it can show them on Home. A globe sticker is a small copy of a photo's subject, cut out on this device. A trip video is made on the device when you tap Save or Share, and the file is deleted when you close the screen.

To show Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, Moondeuk keeps small, downsized copies of a few photos from the trips each day will show over the coming week, along with the city name and trip dates and a globe image drawn by the app, in an app group container on this device (a place only Moondeuk and its widgets can read). Locked and hidden trips are not included. These are erased by Delete All Records and are not included in backups.

3. When data leaves your device — four cases

① Finding place names (everyone)

Coordinates alone don't say "Busan" or "Kyoto", so Moondeuk asks Apple's location service (geocoding) about representative coordinates.

  • It does not ask about each photo — one representative coordinate per city
  • In South Korea, if photos grouped as one city span two cities or counties, it also asks about one representative coordinate per area of about 5 km where photos gather, so region names match where the photos were taken. Each area is asked only once and remembered on the device
  • Places detected as your home area are also asked about once each, to show a neighborhood name. Places in the list of major world cities built into the app are not asked about; the list's name is used
  • City names on tickets in the Globe tab, in trip videos, and in widgets are written in English, so the first time one is drawn, Moondeuk asks once more for the English name, using the same representative coordinate (cities in the built-in list and names already received in English are not asked about)
  • Names, once received, are kept on the device and not asked again
  • No photos are sent
  • This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy

② Maps in trip details and on the back of tickets

When you open a trip or city, an Apple map appears at the top. When you flip a ticket in the Globe tab, an Apple map of the area around where that photo was taken appears. To draw these maps, map tiles for the visible area are downloaded from Apple, so Apple can tell which area's map was requested.

  • Photo markers are placed on the map on the device — no photos are sent
  • The globes in the Globe tab, trip videos, and widgets are drawn from map data built into the app. Drawing the globe uses no network (only English city names are asked about, as in ①)
  • This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy

③ iCloud backup (only if you back up)

The contents described in section 2 are sent to your own iCloud. This includes the coordinates of places you traveled. The app talks to iCloud directly, never through a developer server.

  • Backups are not automatic. One is uploaded each time you tap Back Up Now in Me › Backup
  • Restore downloads the backup from your iCloud and replaces the records on this device
  • When you first open Moondeuk on a new iPhone, it checks once whether your iCloud has a Moondeuk backup, and if so asks whether to restore it

④ Sharing (only when you tap Share)

When you tap Share, an image or video newly drawn on the device is handed to the iOS share sheet, and the app you choose (Messages, social apps, Save Image, and so on) receives it. There are three things you can share:

  • Ticket (shown when you tap a photo in the Globe tab) — the photo, the names of the city and country where it was taken (in English), the trip's dates, length, and number of photos, and the day the photo was taken
  • Trip Video (from Trip Video in trip details) — up to eight photos from the trip, the names of the cities visited (in English) and the route on a globe drawn by the app, the trip's dates, which day of the trip it is, the day each photo was taken, and a ticket at the end. There is no sound
  • Year in Review — by default without photos, only passport stamps and numbers. Only if you turn on Include Photos are up to four stamped photos included

For all three:

  • No photo location (GPS) or other original metadata is included. It is a newly drawn image or video, not the original file. Capture time is shown only to the day
  • Photos, city names, and routes of locked trips are never included
  • Moondeuk does not send it anywhere itself or keep a copy. How the receiving app handles it follows that app's policy

Nothing else leaves your device. Nothing is ever sent to a developer server.

4. Purchases

Moondeuk Premium (monthly and yearly subscriptions, and Lifetime) is paid through the App Store and processed by Apple.

  • Payment methods and details are handled by Apple; Moondeuk never receives them
  • The app only checks, on the device, which product you have from the purchase records Apple provides
  • The developer receives only sales totals from Apple — not who bought (name, contact, or Apple Account)
  • You can cancel subscriptions and request refunds in iPhone Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions, or through Apple

5. On This Day notifications

A Premium feature that sends you, in the evening, a photo from a trip years ago today (or that week). Tapping it opens that trip's video.

  • These are local notifications scheduled on the device. There is no server sending them
  • The small photo in a notification is a reduced copy made on the device and kept with iOS notifications. It may appear on the Lock Screen
  • Photos from locked trips are never chosen for notifications
  • Turn them off in Me › On This Day Notifications, or in Settings › Apps › Moondeuk › Notifications

6. Trip Lock and device authentication

When you lock a trip, opening it requires Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode.

  • iOS performs the check. The app only learns "passed / not passed" and cannot access face or fingerprint data
  • Moondeuk never creates or stores a separate password — with no stored secret, there is nothing to leak
  • Locked trips never appear on Home, the Globe, in notifications, widgets, share images, trip videos, or counts

7. Third parties

None.

  • Moondeuk contains no advertising SDKs, analytics tools, or crash reporters, and does not track you across other apps or websites
  • Data is not sold or shared
  • Cases ①–④ in section 3 go to Apple's services, your own iCloud, or apps you choose; the developer never receives your data
  • If you turned on "Share With App Developers" in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements, Apple may pass crash logs to the developer. These contain no photos or travel records, and nothing is passed if you turn it off

8. Retention and deletion

Data on your device is kept until you delete it. The developer keeps no data, so there is nothing to delete on the developer's side.

Deleting the app also deletes the data on the device. Your iCloud backup must be deleted separately as above.

There is no retention period after deletion. The developer keeps no data, so once you delete it, it is gone. A backup copy exists only in your own iCloud, so deleting it leaves nothing behind.

Moondeuk is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
